Chicken Biryani

45 min • Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken, cut into pieces
  • 3 cups basmati rice, soaked 30 min
  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced and fried
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2-3 green chilies, slit
  • Fresh coriander and mint leaves
  • Biryani masala, salt, and cooking oil to taste
  • Saffron or food color soaked in warm milk

Instructions

  1. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, and biryani masala for at least 30 minutes.
  2. In a large pot, fry sliced onions until golden and set aside for garnish.
  3. In the same pot, cook the marinated chicken with tomatoes until the oil separates.
  4. Parboil the soaked rice with whole spices until 70% cooked, then drain.
  5. Layer the rice over the chicken masala, top with fried onions, mint, coriander, and saffron milk.
  6. Cover and cook on low heat (dum) for 20-25 minutes until fully done.
  7. Gently mix before serving and enjoy hot with raita or salad.

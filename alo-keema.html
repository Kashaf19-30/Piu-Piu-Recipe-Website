Aloo Keema

Cooking Time: 45–55 minutes Servings: 4 persons

Ingredients

  • 500 g minced meat (keema)
  • 3 medium potatoes
  • 2 large onions (finely chopped)
  • 2 tomatoes (chopped)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 green chilies (chopped)
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 cup fresh coriander leaves
  • 3 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 cup water
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Wash and peel the potatoes, then cut them into medium-sized cubes.
  2. Heat oil in a pot and fry the chopped onions until golden brown.
  3. Add cumin seeds and ginger-garlic paste. Cook for 1–2 minutes.
  4. Add the minced meat and cook until it changes color and the water dries up.
  5. Add chopped tomatoes, green chilies, red chili powder, turmeric, coriander powder, and salt.
  6. Cook the mixture until the tomatoes become soft and the oil starts separating.
  7. Add the potato cubes and mix well with the keema.
  8. Add 1 cup of water, cover the pot, and cook on medium heat until the potatoes become tender.
  9. Remove the lid and cook for a few more minutes until the gravy reaches your desired consistency.
  10. Add garam masala and black pepper, then mix gently.
  11. Garnish with fresh coriander and green chilies.
  12. Serve hot with naan, roti, or steamed rice.

Chicken Karahi

Ingredients:

  • 1 kg chicken (cut into pieces)
  • 5 medium tomatoes (chopped)
  • 2 medium onions (finely chopped)
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 4–5 green chilies
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp crushed black pepper
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 cup fresh coriander leaves
  • 2 tbsp fresh ginger (julienne)
  • 1/2 cup cooking oil
  • Salt to taste

Instructions:

  1. Heat oil in a karahi or large cooking pan over medium-high heat.
  2. Add the chicken pieces and cook for 5–7 minutes until they are lightly browned.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
  4. Add chopped tomatoes, salt, red chili powder, turmeric, coriander powder, and cumin seeds.
  5. Mix everything well and cover the karahi. Cook for 10–15 minutes until the tomatoes become soft.
  6. Remove the lid and cook on medium-high heat until the tomato mixture becomes thick and the oil starts separating.
  7. Add green chilies and crushed black pepper.
  8. Continue cooking until the chicken is completely tender and well coated with the tomato gravy.
  9. Add garam masala and mix gently.
  10. Garnish with fresh coriander and sliced ginger.
  11. Cook for another 2–3 minutes and turn off the heat.
  12. Serve hot with naan, roti, or chapati.
  • Cooking Time: 45–55 minutes
  • Servings: 4–5 persons
